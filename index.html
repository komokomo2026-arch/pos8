About Us

Simple sales and stock management for your business.

EKAMBANI POS helps businesses manage their daily sales and products in an easy and organized way.

What you can do
  • Add and manage your products, with prices and stock levels
  • Record sales quickly, search for an item or scan its barcode
  • Print invoices and keep track of who made each sale
  • See daily sales, profit and stock value on your Home page
  • Create reports by month, year, item or payment method

Your information is saved on your own device, so the app is quick and simple to use. We built EKAMBANI POS to make everyday record-keeping less stressful for small and growing businesses.

Privacy Policy

Short, clear and honest.

Your data stays with you

EKAMBANI POS saves your products, barcodes, sales, customer details, receipts and business settings on your own device, inside your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). We do not upload this information to a server.

What is stored
  • Product details, prices, stock and optional barcodes
  • Sales records, including customer name and phone number if you type them in
  • Your business name, contact details and the name shown as "Sold by"
You are in control

You can view, edit or delete your records inside the app at any time. Clearing your browser's site data will also remove everything stored by the app on that device, so keep that in mind before you do it.

Camera

The camera is used only when you tap a scanner icon to read a barcode or QR code. Images are not saved or sent anywhere. If you decline camera access, the app keeps working and you can type codes by hand.

Contact information

If you choose to contact us or request an upgrade through WhatsApp, the details you enter (such as your business name and phone number) are shared only through that WhatsApp message, and only because you sent them.

No unnecessary collection

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect personal information we do not need.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page.
